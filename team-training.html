Questions teams ask

How many people can you train at once?

Team sessions work best with up to 15 people so everyone gets hands-on help. For bigger teams, sessions are split into groups on the same day or across a few days.

Do we need any technical background?

No. Sessions start from zero. If someone can write an email, they can do this.

Which AI tools do you teach?

Mostly ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot and Canva's AI features, plus whatever your team already pays for. The call before the session decides which ones fit.

What does the Slack support include?

Your team gets a private channel. Post a question, a screenshot or a draft, and Andrew answers within one business day. It's for questions about using AI in your work, not a help desk for unrelated software.

Where do sessions happen?

At your office anywhere in DFW. If you don't have a room that fits, a coworking space conference room can be booked for you.

Can real estate agents in a brokerage join if they're independent contractors?

Yes. Many brokerages offer training as a perk for their agents. Talk to Andrew about splitting the cost or running it as an agent event.
