An AI policy your team will actually read.

Your employees are already using AI, approved or not. A short, plain-English policy tells them which tools are OK, what data stays out, and when a human has to check the work.

Talk about an AI policy

Why it matters now

Without rules, people make their own. Someone pastes a client's financials into a free chatbot. Someone sends an AI-written email with a made-up fact in it. Nobody meant any harm, and nobody told them otherwise.

A policy isn't about slowing people down. It's what lets you say yes to AI with confidence.

What your policy covers

  • Approved AI tools and which accounts to use them on
  • Data that never goes into AI: client info, financials, passwords, contracts
  • When AI output needs a human review before it's sent or published
  • How to disclose AI use to clients when it matters
  • Who owns the policy and how it gets updated

How it gets done

Find out what's happening

A short survey and conversation to learn which AI tools your team already uses and for what.

Write the policy

A two-to-three page policy in plain language, written for your business and industry, reviewed with your leadership.

Train the team on it

The policy is walked through in person so people understand the why, not just the rules.

Included in every Team Program. Also available on its own from $2,500. This is practical guidance for small businesses, not legal advice; your attorney should review anything that touches regulated data.
