How it works.

Every session starts with you: what you do, what you already know, and where your time goes. Then Andrew shows you, side by side, how AI fits into that.

Start with your work

Before anything is scheduled, Andrew talks with you about your role, your business and what a normal week looks like. Nothing is generic. The session is shaped around those answers.

Learn shoulder to shoulder

You work on your own laptop, on your own real work, with Andrew right there. When something doesn't click, you get help in the moment instead of rewinding a video.

Keep going afterward

Questions show up once you're back at your desk. You'll have a direct line to Andrew while the new habits settle in, so you aren't left on your own.

What you walk away with

The goal isn't to learn every AI tool. It's to leave with a few ways of working you'll actually keep using, because they came from your own work.

  • A clear sense of what AI is good at, and where it isn't
  • The confidence to use it on your own, without a script
  • Ways to get answers that sound like you and fit your business
  • Habits for checking AI's work against what you know
  • A short plan for where to take it next

Three settings

With your team

At your office

Andrew comes to you and works with your whole team on the work they actually do. Everyone learns together, so AI becomes part of how the business runs, not one person's side project.

In a small group

An owner and a few key people

A focused session for you and the people you rely on most. Good when you want to figure out where AI fits before bringing in everyone else.

At an open session

Hosted around DFW

Small sessions at coworking spaces across the metroplex for individuals who want to learn on their own. Same approach, with a handful of other local professionals at the table.
