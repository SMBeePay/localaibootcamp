Bring your whole team along.

Your people are already experimenting with AI on their own, with mixed results. Training together gives everyone the same footing, the same habits and the same guardrails.

Talk about your team

Built around how your team works

Before any training, Andrew learns how your business runs: the roles, the busy seasons, the tasks that eat up time. The sessions are then built around your team's real work, so people leave using AI on the things they'll do tomorrow.

  • In-person sessions at your office
  • Hands-on time for every person, on their own work
  • Ongoing support after training while habits settle in
  • Clear guidelines for how your company uses AI

Guidelines your team will actually follow

Your team is already using AI, approved or not. Without guidance, people make their own rules about what's safe to share and what needs a second look.

Andrew helps you put simple, plain-English AI guidelines in place: which tools are approved, what information stays out, and when a person needs to review AI's work before it goes out. Then he walks your team through them in person, so they understand the why.

Your AI guidelines cover

  • Which AI tools your team can use, and how
  • Client and company information that never goes into AI
  • When AI output needs a human review
  • Who to ask when something isn't covered

Practical guidance for your business, not legal advice.

Questions teams ask

How is this different from an online AI course?

Online courses are the same for everyone, and most people never finish them. This is built around your team's actual work, delivered in person, with Andrew in the room to help each person as they go.

Does my team need to be technical?

No. Most people start with little or no AI experience. If someone can write an email, they can do this.

Will AI replace people on my team?

That's not what this is about. The focus is helping the people you already have use AI to get more out of their own experience and skills.

What does it cost?

Every engagement is shaped around your team: how many people, what they do and what support you want afterward. Start a conversation and Andrew will put together a proposal.

Where does training happen?

At your office anywhere in DFW. If you don't have a room that fits, a meeting space can be arranged nearby.

Can a brokerage offer this to its agents?

Yes. Many brokerages offer training as a benefit for their agents. Andrew can work with you on how to set it up.
