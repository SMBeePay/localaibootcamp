Hi, I’m Andrew.

I’ve spent well over 15 years in marketing. Lately most of that work runs through AI.

Background

I’m Andrew Neal, and I live here in DFW. I’ve helped lead marketing for a nine-figure service business, an online education company, a fintech startup and an e-commerce brand. I’ve managed seven-figure budgets, sales teams and content teams. I’ve also been on the owner’s side: I run my own service business here in DFW, and AI handles a big part of the marketing and back office that keep it going.

Building with AI

I don’t just use AI tools; I build with them. I’ve led AI initiatives for clients and inside the companies I’ve worked with. That includes building AI systems that turned hours of manual work into minutes, and helping launch an AI-powered product to customers who had to trust it before they’d use it. That experience shaped how I teach.

AI is a tool.

It won’t replace what you know about your clients, your market or your craft. It does let you put that knowledge to work faster and in more places. Most people don’t need another webinar. They need someone in the room who can sit down with their real work and show them what’s possible.

Teaching runs in my family

My parents were public school teachers for more than 30 years each, and my sister is in her 24th year in the classroom. I grew up watching how good teachers work: they meet people where they are and stay with them until it clicks. That’s how I run every session.
