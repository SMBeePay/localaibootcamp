Hi, I'm Andrew.

I teach small business owners and their teams how to use AI on the work they already do. In person, at a table, on your own laptop.

Based in Royse City. Training across Dallas, Fort Worth and everywhere in between.

Background

I've spent more than 15 years in marketing. I've been head of marketing for 8-figure service companies and a senior marketing director at both bootstrapped and VC-funded software companies.

I also own and run a local service business here in DFW. AI writes my first drafts, organizes my notes and saves me hours every week, and I've learned where it falls on its face.

Why in person

Online AI courses are everywhere, and most people never finish them. What actually works is sitting next to someone while they try it on their own email, their own listing, their own quote, and fixing what goes wrong right there.

That's what these sessions are. No hype and no jargon, just your work, done faster, with you still in charge of it.
