Hi, I'm Andrew.

I help people take what they already know and make it go further with AI. In person, at the same table, on your own work.

Based in Royse City. Working with people across Dallas, Fort Worth and everywhere in between.

Background

I've spent more than 15 years in marketing. I've been head of marketing for 8-figure service companies and a senior marketing director at both bootstrapped and VC-funded software companies.

I also own and run a local service business here in DFW. AI is part of how I work every day. It hasn't replaced anything I know. It's let me do more with it.

Why in person

Most people I talk to have already tried to learn AI. They've bookmarked the courses, watched the videos and sat through the webinars. What they didn't get was someone showing them how it applies to their job, their clients and their business.

That's what I do. I sit down next to you, we work on your real work, and we figure it out together. You leave more capable than you came in, and still fully in charge.
