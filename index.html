You don't need another course.

There's no shortage of AI content. What's missing is someone showing you how it applies to you.

Online courses

You sign up, watch the first two modules, and never finish. Nobody notices.

YouTube videos

Great demos built on someone else's business. Getting from their example to your work is left to you.

Webinars

An hour of slides, a few takeaways, and nothing changes on Monday morning.

AI doesn't replace what you know. It multiplies it.

Your experience, judgment and relationships are what make you good at your job. AI can't replace those. Used well, it takes the first draft, the research and the repetitive parts off your plate, so you spend more of your time on the work only you can do.

That's the whole idea here. You don't come out of this as a "tech person." You come out as a better version of what you already are, with a new skill you'll use for the rest of your career.

You also learn where AI falls short: when it's confidently wrong, and why your own knowledge is what keeps it honest.
