How team training works

We talk about your work

A 30-minute call about what your team does all day, which tools you already use, and where time goes. The session is built around those answers.

Andrew trains in the room

Your team works on their own laptops with their own emails, listings, quotes and posts. No slide marathons. Everyone leaves with things they made.

Support continues after

Questions come up once people get back to work. Your team posts them in a private Slack channel and gets real answers, not a help article.

Your team is already using AI. Do you have rules for it?

Most small businesses have staff pasting client details into free AI tools with no guidance on what's safe. A short, plain-English AI use policy fixes that, and every team program includes one.

How the AI policy works
  • Which AI tools your team is approved to use
  • What client and company data never goes into AI
  • When AI output needs a human review before it goes out
  • Who to ask when something isn't covered
